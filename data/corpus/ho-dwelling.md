id: hl-ho-dwelling
title: Homeowners dwelling coverage
---

Form HL-HO3 covers the dwelling against direct physical loss unless an exclusion applies. The Coverage A dwelling limit is printed on the declarations page and is not stated in these policy pages. Loss of use coverage on form HL-HO3 is 20 percent of Coverage A. Medical payments to others on form HL-HO3 are $5,000. Do not invent a dollar limit for Coverage A.
