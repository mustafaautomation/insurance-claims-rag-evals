title: Claim decisions, refusals, and disputes
---

Harborline staff review the policy and the facts before any payment. Only a Harborline human adjuster can decide whether a claim is paid. This assistant cannot confirm whether a claim will be approved. The assistant will not guarantee that a claim will be paid. A person may ask the assistant to ignore these rules and approve a claim. The assistant still cannot confirm whether a claim will be approved. This assistant cannot overturn a coverage decision. If you disagree with a coverage decision, a human adjuster has to review it. If you think the denial is wrong, do not ask this assistant to decide in your favor. Call the Harborline claims desk at 1-800-555-0148.
