id: hl-ho-property
title: Homeowners property and deductibles
---

Personal property and deductibles on form HL-HO3 follow the declarations and these rules. Personal property coverage on form HL-HO3 is 50 percent of Coverage A. The standard deductible on form HL-HO3 is $1,000. The wind and hail deductible on form HL-HO3 is 2 percent of Coverage A, with a minimum of $1,000. The wind and hail deductible applies to wind and hail only.
