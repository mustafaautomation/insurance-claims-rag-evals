id: hl-au-rental
title: Rental car reimbursement
---

Rental reimbursement is optional. Endorsement HL-END-RENT pays $40 per day for a rental car, for a maximum of 30 days. It applies only when the declarations list HL-END-RENT and the loss is a covered collision or comprehensive loss. It does not pay for a rental after a mechanical breakdown.
