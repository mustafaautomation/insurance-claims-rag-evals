id: hl-au-uim
title: Uninsured motorist endorsement
---

Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage is optional. Endorsement HL-END-UIM provides uninsured motorist bodily injury coverage of $100,000 per person and $300,000 per accident. The endorsement applies only when the declarations list HL-END-UIM. It does not increase liability limits.
