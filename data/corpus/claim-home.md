title: Home claim steps and proof of loss
---

After a home loss, protect the property from further damage, then call the Harborline claims desk at 1-800-555-0148. List the damaged items and keep receipts. A field inspection may be scheduled. Proof of loss form HL-POL is due within 60 days after Harborline requests it. The form asks for the insured's name, the policy number, the date and cause of the loss, the damaged property, the amounts claimed, and a signature.
