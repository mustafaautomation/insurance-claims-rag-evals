title: How to file an auto claim
---

To open an auto claim, call the Harborline claims desk at 1-800-555-0148. Report an auto loss within 72 hours, or as soon as reasonably possible. Give the date, the location, what happened, and the other parties. Photograph the damage before repairs when it is safe to do so. Do not authorize repairs above $250 until a Harborline appraiser sees the vehicle. Opening a report is not a payment decision.
